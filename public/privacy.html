SoberBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

SoberBuddy ("we", "us", "our") operates the SoberBuddy rider app and the SoberBuddyDriver app (together, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our Services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, phone number, email (if provided), and driver license details for drivers.
  • Identity verification documents (drivers): photos of government ID, driving license, vehicle registration, and related KYC documents you upload during onboarding.
  • Location data: precise location while the app is in use to match riders with drivers, provide navigation, and show trip progress.
  • Trip information: pickup and drop-off addresses, trip status, fare details, and trip history.
  • Device and usage data: device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and secure the Services.
  • Communications: messages or support requests you send to us.

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • Verify driver identity and eligibility.
  • Connect riders with nearby drivers and complete trips.
  • Calculate fares, process payments, and provide trip history.
  • Send trip updates, safety alerts, and service notifications.
  • Improve app performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Permissions

Our apps may request the following device permissions:

  • Location: required for trip booking, driver availability, and navigation.
  • Camera: used when drivers capture profile or verification document photos.
  • Photo library (system picker): used when you choose existing photos for profile or document upload.
  • Notifications: used to send trip and account updates.
  • Internet: required for app functionality.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data:

  • Between riders and drivers to complete trips (for example, name, phone number, and pickup location).
  • With service providers that help us operate the Services (for example, cloud hosting, maps, and authentication).
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of users and the public.

We use Google Firebase and Google Maps as part of our infrastructure.

5. Data retention

We retain account and trip data while your account is active and as needed to provide the Services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal requirements. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices

  • You can control location and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • You can update profile information in the app.
  • You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your data, subject to legal limits.

8. Children

Our Services are not intended for users under 18 years of age.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this URL and update the "Last updated" date above.
